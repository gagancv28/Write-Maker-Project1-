## Design Principles

### Aesthetics: Astra-Digital
- **High-Contrast Glassmorphism:** UI elements feel like transparent HUDs floating over a cosmic void.
- **Luminous Outlines:** Use light and glows instead of traditional shadows to define depth.
- **Precision Spacing:** Strict 8px rhythmic scale.
- **Soft Roundness:** 4px radius for standard components, 8px for cards.
